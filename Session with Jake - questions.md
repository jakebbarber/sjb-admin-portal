# Session with Jake: what to capture for each Admin portal tool

Jake's reply (13 Oct 2026) set the roadmap, for both new business (NB) and existing clients (EC):

1. RWL checks for UK-related business
2. AO1 checks
3. AO2 checks (including FRFs in the US)
4. Apps In checks

Vickey is to share the emails from Tom and Rob, which will show the pattern of objections for each.

The RWL tool exists. The other three are unknown processes until Jake walks through them. Record the session, transcribe it, and make sure these questions get answered for **each** of the four checks. One answer per question is enough; the transcript does the rest.

## For each check

**What it is**
- What does the name stand for, and what stage of the client journey is it? (AO1, AO2, Apps In, FRF: spell them out.)
- Does it apply to new business, existing clients, or both? Is the process different for each?
- UK business only, or cross-border too? What changes for the US / FRFs?

**Who and when**
- Who prepares the pack today (adviser, paraplanner, admin)? Who at Blacktower checks it (Tom, Rob, someone else)?
- When does it happen: before submission, after, or both? What is the typical turnaround?
- How many a week, roughly? How many bounce back?

**Inputs**
- Exactly which documents go in the pack: list them, with the format of each (Word, PDF, scanned, an online form, a spreadsheet).
- Which of them come from templates? Where do the templates live, and who owns them?
- Is there a checklist or a manual section Blacktower checks against? Is it the same Sales Manual, or another document? Get a copy.

**What a pass looks like**
- What does Blacktower actually look for? What are the three most common reasons it comes back?
- What are the hard stops (missing signature, missing ID, a date out of range) versus wording points?
- Any numbers that must reconcile across documents (fees, values, dates, names)?

**The bounce-back**
- How does the objection come back: an email like the RWL one, a form, a tracked-changes document, a portal comment?
- Who fixes it and what does the fix usually involve: re-wording, a missing document, a resubmission of the whole pack?
- Does Blacktower expect tracked changes, a clean version, or a covering reply?

**Outputs**
- What does the admin team need at the end: a corrected document, a checklist report, a reply to Compliance, a status in a log?
- Where does the finished pack go (email, a shared folder, Blacktower's own system)?

## Things to get from Jake or Vickey before building

- Two or three real examples of each check that bounced back, with the compliance reply. We will build fictional test versions from them; the real ones never go into the system.
- Any Blacktower checklist, form or manual section for AO1, AO2, Apps In and FRFs.
- The current templates for each.
- Who should have access to each tool in the Admin portal, and whether any check should be adviser-facing as well.

## What the portal already provides for each new tool

- A sign-in, the Admin portal shell, a rail entry and a Home card (a new tool is one part file).
- Word reading and tracked-change writing, the Sales Manual store and checklist, Claude calls with a fixed JSON shape, chunked file storage, an audit history per case.
- The RWL Review pattern: upload, check, paste the objection, tick the changes, download, copy the reply. Most checks will fit the same four steps.
